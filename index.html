Our Journey

The Brewery · Est. 2020
Tours by appointment

We opened our doors at the worst possible moment, and stayed open anyway. In the spring of 2020, while every chart on every screen was bleeding red, Will was quietly filling kegs in a corner of Summit and figuring out how to hand a stranger a beer six feet at a time.

What started as a hedge against a very strange year turned into something real: a taproom that regulars now treat like a second living room. Every batch is small, brewed on-site, and named for the market that nearly took us out.

We don't chase trophies or trends. We chase the pour that makes a commuter miss one train on purpose. Fifteen years of brewing experience go into a list that stays deliberately short, deliberately fresh, and deliberately ours.

Curious how it's made? We run small, private brewery tours: the tanks, the process, and a few opinions about beer you didn't ask for.
